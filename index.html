About Maintec Financial Advisory

A Coordinated Advisory Ecosystem Designed for Enterprise Growth.

"Integrated Business Solutions. Understand the business first, execute with practical insight."

Maintec Financial Advisory Services Private Limited is an entrepreneur-led business advisory company that connects experience in banking and capital mobilisation with strategic project development, sustainable enterprise initiatives and coordinated professional support. Its leadership brings together more than three decades of banking experience and practical engagement in government-linked projects, rural entrepreneurship and circular-economy development in Andhra Pradesh. The company aims to help businesses navigate financing, growth, organisational requirements and implementation through a coordinated advisory model.

Company History & Incorporation

Maintec Financial Advisory Services Private Limited was incorporated on 20 August 2008 (CIN: U65991KA2008PTC047500, ROC Bangalore). The website positioning reflects the backgrounds of its current entrepreneurial leadership in banking, fund-raising consultancy and sustainable enterprise development.
